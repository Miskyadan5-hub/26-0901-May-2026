About Us

Born on the streets of Nairobi, built for the world. M-Kicks Limited is more than a sneaker brand — it is a cultural movement.

Our Story

Founded in 2026, M-Kicks started with a simple idea — that African streets deserve world-class footwear designed right here at home. We build every pair with pride, purpose, and passion.

Our Mission

To create premium sneakers that celebrate African identity, empower local communities, and compete on the global stage. Quality is not a feature — it is our foundation.

Our Values

Culture, craft, and community drive everything we do. We believe that great footwear starts from the ground up — in design, in materials, and in the people who wear them.

From Nairobi To The World

M-Kicks Limited was founded by Misky Adan with a vision to put African streetwear on the global map. What started as a passion project has grown into a brand reaching 14 countries, with over 2,000 happy customers and a lineup of 8+ models built for style, comfort, and confidence.

We are not just making shoes. We are making history.
